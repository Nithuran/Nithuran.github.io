About

I like problems where the data is messy, the stakeholders are impatient, and the answer actually changes what someone does next.

My background is in QA engineering and infrastructure security at BT, where four years of analysing large-scale operational and vulnerability data — and building the automation to make sense of it — is what pulled me toward data science in the first place. I completed the University of Cambridge's Data Science, Machine Learning & AI Career Accelerator to formalise that shift, working through the full pipeline — cleaning, exploration, modelling, evaluation, and communication — across six independent projects.

About

I like problems where the data is messy and the answer actually changes what someone does next.

I started at BT as a Developer - gathering requirements straight from users, building the features they actually needed, and debugging everything before it shipped to production. That hands-on instinct carried into QA Engineering, where I owned test strategy, automated regression testing, and led sign-off on major releases. Most recently I moved into Infrastructure Security Operations, working with large-scale vulnerability data to decide what gets fixed and when.

Data science felt like the natural next step. It's the same instinct as before: take messy, high-volume data and turn it into something someone can act on. I completed the University of Cambridge's Data Science, Machine Learning & AI Career Accelerator to formalise that shift, working through six independent projects end to end.

I move comfortably between classical statistics and modern ML - SARIMAX to XGBoost to transformer time-series models. I care as much about explaining why a model works as making it perform well. I'm now looking for a data science or analytics role where I can do exactly that.
